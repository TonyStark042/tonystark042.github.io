👋 About Me

Hello, I am Shuai Zhen (甄帅), a master's student in Computer Science and Technology at Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT). I received my Bachelor's degree from Tiangong University in 2024.

My research interests lie in Reinforcement Learning (RL) and Large Language Models (LLMs), with a particular focus on:

  1. Improving the sample and training efficiency of RL for LLMs.
  2. Exploring novel applications of RL to enhance LLM capabilities.
  3. Developing more efficient and effective attention mechanisms.
